# C7 Vertebra Prominens Anatomical Anchor and Biomechanical Motion

## 1. Osteological Landmark
The spinous process of the seventh cervical vertebra (C7, vertebra prominens) is the most prominent palpable bony landmark at the cervicothoracic junction in the posterior midline. In flexion of the cervical spine, C7 remains relatively fixed while C6 displaces anteriorly.

## 2. Palpation and Surface Anatomy
In standard neutral posture, the distance from the cutaneous surface to the tip of the C7 spinous process ranges between 8.5 mm and 14.2 mm depending on subcutaneous adipose thickness.
