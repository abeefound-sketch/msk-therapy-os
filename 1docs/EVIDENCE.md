# Evidence and License Register (Milestone M1)

Every substantive educational statement, anatomical mapping, safety threshold, and red flag rule in MSK-Therapy-OS references a vetted entry in this evidence catalog.

## Evidence Tier Definitions

- **Tier 1 (Clinical Guidelines / Systematic Reviews / Regulators):** Highest standard for clinical red flags, contraindications, and escalation rules.
- **Tier 2 (Peer-Reviewed Anatomy & Reference Atlases):** Established morphological definitions, 3D coordinate meshes, and joint kinematics.
- **Tier 3 (Manufacturer Manuals & Device Documentation):** Machine specifications, waveforms, current limits, and electrode configurations. (Device parameter recommendations remain locked pending verified manual and regulatory clearance).
- **Tier 4 (Traditional Practice & Cultural Schemas):** Cultural/traditional educational schemas (Sen Sib lines, Dien Chan facial points). Presented with explicit disclaimers; no established physical equivalence to nerves or vessels.

## Clinical Safety and Efficacy Notice

**No therapeutic efficacy claim is made by this system.** Educational outcomes require valid pedagogical assessment designs. All clinical interventions in practice require formal licensing, institutional governance, and qualified practitioner oversight.
