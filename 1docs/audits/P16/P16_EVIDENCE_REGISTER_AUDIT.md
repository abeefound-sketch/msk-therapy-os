## 1. Evidence Classification Framework

Milestone P16 structures all 20 scientific citations (EV-01 through EV-20) under formal evidence categories and confidence classifications:

1. **`MODEL_BASED_MECHANICS`:** Grounded in computational multi-body dynamics, static equilibrium, or physical conservation laws.
2. **`ANATOMICAL_EVIDENCE`:** Derived from direct cadaveric dissection, bone pin pins, or histological spindle quantification.
3. **`OBSERVATIONAL_ASSOCIATION`:** Cross-sectional or cohort associations without established causality.
4. **`INTERVENTION_EVIDENCE`:** Randomized controlled trials or controlled experimental exercise interventions.
5. **`UNVALIDATED_HYPOTHESIS`:** Conceptual clinical models lacking prospective empirical confirmation.

## 3. Epistemological Governance

The evidence register explicitly distinguishes between mechanistic physics (EV-01, EV-09, EV-10) and epidemiological pain correlations (EV-17, EV-18, EV-19). This prevents the common fallacy of assuming that increased mechanical moment arm automatically constitutes a clinical disease entity.
